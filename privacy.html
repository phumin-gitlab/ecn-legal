Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how ECN Publisher ("the app"), an internal tool operated by the team behind the Facebook Page "โดนตัวนี้อีกแล้ว", handles data.

1. Data we access

  • When our own TikTok account authorizes the app, TikTok gives us an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) under the user.info.basic scope.
  • The video.upload scope lets the app send our video files to that account's drafts.

2. How we use it

Only to upload our own videos to our own account and confirm the upload worked. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a local configuration file that is not published. We do not run a server and do not collect data from other TikTok users or visitors of this site.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens expire automatically and are deleted when no longer needed. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: message our Facebook Page.
