ECN Publisher

ECN Publisher is a small internal tool used by the team behind the Facebook Page "โดนตัวนี้อีกแล้ว" and the TikTok account @everythingcheapna. It uploads short product-review videos that we made ourselves to our own TikTok account as drafts, so we can review them in the TikTok app before posting.

It is not offered to the public and is used only with accounts we own.
